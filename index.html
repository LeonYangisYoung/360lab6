What we are into

Action Games

Fast games that keep you moving and thinking.

Why we like it

Action games are exciting, especially when you learn a new skill or beat a difficult level.

RPG Adventures

Explore new worlds and follow interesting stories.

Why we like it

We enjoy making choices, meeting characters and seeing how a story changes.

Indie Games

Small games with creative ideas and styles.

Why we like it

Indie games often try something unusual and feel different from big releases.

Multiplayer

Play with friends or meet new teammates.

Why we like it

Playing with other people makes even simple games more fun.

Game Development

Learn how games are made behind the scenes.

Why we like it

It is interesting to see how art, sound, code and design work together.

Gaming Events

Join casual meetups, game nights and discussions.

Why we like it

Events are relaxed. You can play, watch, talk or just hang out.
